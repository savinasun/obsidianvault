## **Internal Covariate Shift**

**Tags**:: #dl_theo
**Links**:: [[Normalization]] · [[Layer Normalization]] · [[Neural Network Initialization]]

---
- Internal covariate shift ([Ioffe & Szegedy, 2015](https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.03167)) refers to **the phenomenon where the distribution of inputs to a deep neural network changes as the network's weights are updated during training**.
- It is something we want to avoid because it would make training process slower.
