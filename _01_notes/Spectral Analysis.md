### Singular Value Decomposition
$$
A = U\Sigma V^T \in \mathbb{R}^{m\times n}
$$
+ $U\in\mathbb{R}^{m\times m}$: matrix of left singular vectors
+ $V\in\mathbb{R}^{n\times n}$: matrix of right singular vectors
+ $A\in\mathbb{R}^{m\times n}$: matrix of left singular vectors
